01 — OVERVIEW

Project Overview

ShopEase is an e-commerce application prototype designed to provide users with a simple and convenient online shopping experience. Users can explore products, search by name, filter categories and manage their shopping cart.

🎯

Project Goal

Create an easy-to-use shopping interface with smooth navigation.

👥

Target Audience

Online shoppers looking for a simple product discovery experience.

💡

Design Approach

Clean layouts, clear navigation and interactive components.

02 — RESEARCH

Problem Statement

Some online shopping interfaces can feel confusing when product information, navigation and cart controls are not presented clearly.

Challenges

  • Finding products quickly
  • Browsing different categories
  • Understanding product details
  • Managing shopping cart items
  • Completing checkout easily

Our Solutions

  • Simple search functionality
  • Category-based filtering
  • Clear product detail popup
  • Interactive shopping cart
  • Simple demo checkout form
